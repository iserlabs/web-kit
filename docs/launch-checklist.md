# Launch checklist

Every site built on the kit ships all ten before its first launch, and every live site is held to
them. `web-kit audit --tier required` proves items 1, 2, 5 (no tracker before consent), 6, 7, 8,
9 and 10 on the rendered pages; items 3 and 4 have their own checks below.

| # | Item | The standard | How it is proven |
|---|------|--------------|------------------|
| 1 | Privacy Policy | `/privacy`, text generated by `privacyPolicy()` from `@iserlabs/web-kit/legal` with the site's own facts | page returns 200; footer link on every page |
| 2 | Terms and Conditions | `/terms`, text from `termsAndConditions()` | page returns 200; footer link on every page |
| 3 | Secrets off the frontend | no API key, token or private key in client code or a `NEXT_PUBLIC_` variable; publishable keys (Stripe `pk_`, a referrer-locked Maps key) are the only exception | grep the repo and the built `.next/static` for the shapes in `checks/launch.mjs`; the audit scans every rendered page |
| 4 | Force HTTPS | `http://` answers with a 301 or 308 to `https://`, and the site sends `Strict-Transport-Security` | `curl -sI http://<domain>` and `curl -sI https://<domain> \| grep -i strict` |
| 5 | Cookie notice | only when the site uses analytics or ad tools that set cookies (`analytics.consentCookies`); a small bar, never a modal, Accept and Decline equal, Global Privacy Control honored, nothing loads before Accept, "Cookie settings" footer link brings it back. A site with no tracking cookies has no notice and says so in its policy | audit flags any tracker script in the server HTML |
| 6 | Titles and descriptions | a distinct `<title>` and meta description on every page | SEO check |
| 7 | Link preview image | `og:image` on every page, the real brand, judged by its worst crop | audit plus the brand pack boards |
| 8 | Favicon | the real logo mark, `icon` plus `apple-touch-icon` | audit |
| 9 | Sitemap and robots.txt | `sitemap.xml` lists every public page, including `/privacy` and `/terms`; `robots.txt` points to it | audit |
| 10 | Alt text | every `<img>` has `alt`: a plain description, or `alt=""` when decorative | SEO check |

## Legal pages, the rules

1) One config per site, written once, in the site repo (for example `src/content/legal.ts`):
   brand, legal name, domain, contact email, governing state, effective date, what the forms ask,
   booking engine, payment processor, whether the site texts people, the services that touch data,
   and the analytics in use. Every value is the site's own fact. Never invent an entity suffix
   ("LLC"); when the legal name is unknown, the brand name stands in.
2) No street address and no phone number on either page. Email is the contact channel.
3) The pages render in the site's own type and layout: one `h1`, the effective date under it, a
   short `h2` per section, a reading column of about 65 characters. They inherit the site header
   and footer. They carry their own title and description, and `robots: index, follow`.
4) The footer link is small and quiet: "Privacy" and "Terms" (and "Cookie settings" when there is a
   notice), on every page, in the footer's smallest type.
5) Health care sites set `healthcare: true`, so the site policy defers to the practice's Notice of
   Privacy Practices.
6) A change to the facts (a new form field, a new analytics tool, a new booking engine) updates the
   config and the effective date in the same commit.
